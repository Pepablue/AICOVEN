Refund Policy

Last updated: 10th November 2026

We want you to feel confident when you book your seat in the AI Coven 3-Day Masterclass. This policy explains how refunds work.

80% refund before your class starts

If you ask for a refund before Day 1 of your cohort starts, you will receive an 80% refund of the amount you paid. On the $27 price, that is $21.60.

No refund once class starts

Once Day 1 of your cohort has started, refunds are 0%.

How to request a refund

  • Email aicoven.support@gmail.com from the email address you used to book.
  • Include your full name and your booking code (it looks like AIC-XXXXXXXXXX and is in your confirmation email).
  • The date and time of your request email decides which refund applies.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds are sent to the original payment method through our payment provider, Paystack.
  • Refunds are paid within 5 business days.
  • Payments and refunds are in US dollars (USD). The amount you see in your own currency may differ slightly because of exchange rates or bank fees.

If we cancel

If we cannot run your class, we will offer you a new date or a full refund.

If you are removed from class

If you are removed for not following the class rules, no refund is given. Examples: keeping your camera off without permission, disrupting class with a noisy background, showing sexual or explicit content, or using vulgar language or being violent, political or racist. Please see our Terms and Conditions.

Questions

Contact us at aicoven.support@gmail.com and we will help.
