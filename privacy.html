Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10th November 2026

AI Coven ("we", "us", "our") runs the AI Coven 3-Day Masterclass at aicoven.online. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Booking details: your name, email address, phone number (with country code), country, age and language, which you give us when you book.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Paystack. We do not see or store your full card or payment details. We receive confirmation of your payment, the amount and your contact details.
  • Messages: anything you send us when you contact us.
  • Technical data: basic information such as your browser, device and approximate location, which our hosting and payment providers may collect automatically.

How we use your information

  • To process your booking and give you access to the masterclass.
  • To email you your Zoom details, reminders and replays.
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds.
  • To keep records and meet legal obligations.
  • To send you news about AI Coven only if you have agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Live sessions and recordings

Our masterclass sessions are held on Zoom and are recorded. Your camera must be on during class. Recordings capture the screen, class materials, what is built, and voices, so your voice can be heard. We hide faces and names in recordings and replays. Replays are shared with attendees and stay online for 6 months. We keep recordings and class work indefinitely. If you have a real reason to keep your camera off, email us before class and we will talk with you.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services we use to run the masterclass. These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Sheets and Gmail): stores our booking records and receives your support emails.
  • Paystack: takes your payment.
  • Zoom: runs the live sessions and recordings.
  • Klaviyo: sends your confirmation, reminder and replay emails.
  • TikTok pixel: helps us measure our ads.

We may also share information when the law requires it.

Cookies and advertising

Our website does not set its own cookies. We use the TikTok pixel to measure our ads, and where the law requires it, the pixel only runs after you accept it in our cookie banner. Our payment provider may also use cookies or similar tools. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep booking and payment records for as long as needed to run the masterclass, handle refunds and meet legal and accounting requirements, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the UK, EU and US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Email aicoven.support@gmail.com and we will respond within 2 business days.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service is completely secure.

Children

Our masterclass is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

International transfers

We are based in Kenya, and our providers may process information in other countries, including the UK and US. By booking, you understand your information may be transferred outside your country.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.
